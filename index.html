Our story

Started by a 12‑year‑old.

FlamingoLacrosse.com was started by a 12-year-old boy. He sells corner targets for lacrosse players who want to shoot better, and every set can be made your way.

How it works

Hang, shoot, dodge.

  1. 1

    Hang

    Hang your two targets in the corners you want to work on. Swap them to the other corners as you improve.

  2. 2

    Shoot

    Say the corner out loud, then shoot at it. Calling it first keeps you from just throwing it at the goalie.

  3. 3

    Dodge

    Dodge hard, pick a corner, then shoot. Practice it the way you play it in a game.

The goal

Four places that count.

A field lacrosse goal is 6 ft wide and 6 ft tall. Your two targets, each 9 inches by 9 inches, go in any two of the four corners, where shots are hardest to save. Tap a corner to see where one would hang. The diagram is not to scale.

Drills

Four ways to use a set.

  • Call your shotPick one of your two targets, say it out loud, then shoot at it. Count how many you hit.10 shots
  • Top shelfHang both targets in the top corners. Shoot from 10 yards.20 shots
  • Low to highHang one low and one high, on opposite sides. Alternate between them.5 rounds
  • Bounce it inHang both targets low and hit them off one bounce. Keep your shot low and hard.10 shots

Customize

Make them yours.

Each set comes with two targets, 9 inches by 9 inches each. Pick a target, choose black, white or gray and add up to three characters, like a jersey number or initials, or add a flamingo or the Florida outline. Hang them in any corners you like.
